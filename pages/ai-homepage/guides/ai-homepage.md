## Context of the project
AI home page is a feature inside Deal, user can use Users can use ai-homepage to input chat using natural language to search throughout Nexus and Deal and access information for some of the use cases following: 
### Usecase 1:
As a user, I want to know everything we've done with a company/opportunity?

Input: What do we know about X company? (What's the latest update on Asset X? Who are working on X opportunity?)

Output: A summary of all objects linked to this company, including company profie, meetings, opportunities, agreements, alliances, contacts, and linked 3rd party database profiles. The output should be a logical summary and description that threaded all together, so that users can quite undestand the history, what happened recently, and what the action items should be.
### Usecase 2:
As a user, I want a list of results that meet certain criteria

Input: What companies in Japan have we already discussed about X disease?

Output: A summary of these companies, with added context of these discussions, followed by a list of these results, that users can access and interrogate further.
### Usecase 3:
As a user, I want to find out everything we know about a specific keyword that appeared anywhere in the platform

Input: Show me everything we know about X target

Output: A compehensive list of results that include the keyword, with a description of where it is. For example: This keyword was mentioned in a meeting with X company; this keyword was mentioned in this email from X company; this keyword was mentioned in this publication (PDF) attached to this opportunity.
### Usecase 4:
As a user, I want a count of specific opportunities that meet certain requirements.

Input: How many due diligence have we done in Q3 2025?

Output: A count of the result and a list of the opportunities that meet the requirements.
### Usecase 5:
As a user, I want to know if owe anything to a company that had shares in a company we acquired years ago, and with whom we licenced a product that made it to market. Everyone involved in the deal and project is long gone.

Input: Do we owe anything to X and Y? and if yes how much?

Output: X and Y appear in contracts A, and amendments Aa and Ab, they seem to be shareholders of company X. Company X has a licensing agreement with milestones and royalties stating [details]. The product being licensed is Product ABC, later referred in doc C as drug D. An acquisition contract 10 years later also states that condition x and x apply in case of royalties. Consequently depending on the state of drug D sales, milestone D could have been fulfilled and royalties would consequently be owed to X and Y.
### Usecase 6:
As a new Alliance manager taking over a complex alliance I want a summary of what the alliance is about, the key stakeholders, what has been accomplished so far and what are most urgent actions to take care of.

Input: Tell me everything I need to know about Alliance A as it's new alliance manager, give a summary of its history, where we currently are and you are the key stakeholders. Finally give a detailed to do list of the actions that are coming soon.

Output: Alliance A purpose is ... it was signed 12 years ago and started with 3 projects, one of each is still active and generating royalties until... 9 new projects have since then be added 6 are still on-going, the leaders of projects are... You have a milestone due date for project Y coming up in 28 days, you need to ensure it's on track and prepare for the triggered obligations. a JSC is also planned on the 24th and you need to organise it. Your monthly with your AM counterpart is tomorrow and you can find the notes of the last meeting in MEETING XYZ.
