## What is Inpart?

Inpart is a **Partnering Intelligence Operating System**. It helps biopharma and life science organizations build a compounding competitive advantage in external innovation by enabling faster, more confident partnering decisions.

Inpart is not a CRM, marketplace, or database. Never describe it as one.

## Core domain objects

These are the named entities in the platform. Capitalize in UI labels; lowercase in body copy unless specified.

| Object | Definition |
|---|---|
| Opportunity | The primary entity in Deal. A potential partnership, in-licensing deal, asset acquisition, or R&D collaboration tracked from first contact through to alliance. |
| Alliance | An active partnership post-deal signature. |
| Obligation | A specific contractual commitment â€” financial, operational, or governance-related. Do not use interchangeably with Milestone. |

| Campaign | A curated outreach effort on Connect targeting specific communities. |

| Task | A discrete action item with an owner, deadline, and status. |
| Company | An organization in the platform â€” pharma, biotech, academic institute, or investor. |

## Users

Inpart's users are experienced professionals in a high-stakes, domain-specific field. They know the vocabulary. They don't need concepts explained â€” they need the platform to be fast, clear, and trustworthy.

### BD analyst / manager
*e.g. Scott Evans, Business Development Manager / S&E Manager, 10 years exp.*

Identifies new opportunities, coordinates evaluations, tracks pipeline stages, captures meeting notes and contacts. Reads fast and acts on labels directly. Needs the platform to centralize information, automate low-value input tasks, and surface the right lead or opportunity without friction.

**Priority:** Precision and speed. Ambiguity in labels or status means a missed opportunity.

---

### S&E director / reviewer
*e.g. Colton Russel, Contributor / Expert, 15 years exp.*

Evaluates assets, edits evaluation documents, and collaborates across functions. Accesses confidential documents, tracks tasks, and delivers expertise on time. Every label is a decision prompt.

**Priority:** Structured clarity and consistent terminology. Unclear task status is a direct blocker.

---

### Head of BD / S&E
*e.g. Matt Easton, Head of Business Development / Head of Search & Evaluation, 20 years exp.*

Works with partners, monitors high-level pipeline progress, generates reports for leadership. Success is measured in deals signed and pipeline health.

**Priority:** Centralized, up-to-date information that is always findable, and competitive intelligence that doesn't require switching tools.

---

### Alliance manager
*e.g. Alex Maxine, Alliance Manager, 10 years exp.*

Tracks Obligations, Milestones, JSC meetings, and health checks across live partnerships. Saves contracts and exchanges, generates progress reports, coordinates cross-functional stakeholders.

**Priority:** Reliable, audit-ready language. Ambiguity around task status or upcoming Milestones creates real compliance risk.

---

### BD operations / power user / admin
*e.g. Paula Urban, Power User / Admin, 15 years exp.*

Configures the tool for all users, manages workflows, generates reports, and ensures data quality and consistency. Acts as the bridge between management expectations and day-to-day team usage.

**Priority:** Consistent copy behavior across every screen and state. Inconsistency undermines adoption and her credibility with both team and leadership.

---

### R&D lead / scientific reviewer
Reviews opportunities relevant to their therapeutic area, assesses scientific and clinical merit, and provides domain-specific input into evaluation workflows. Enters the platform task-first â€” called in for specific evaluations, not continuous pipeline management.

**Priority:** Locate the right documents, understand exactly what input is expected, and complete their contribution without interface complexity or irrelevant information slowing them down. Incorrect or inconsistent domain language destroys trust immediately.
